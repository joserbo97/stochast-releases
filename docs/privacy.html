Stochast

Privacy

Soulbridge Corp · last updated 21 November 2026

Your workbooks stay on your Mac

Stochast runs entirely on your Mac. It reads and writes only the workbook you are working in, and only when you ask it to (adding a cell to a model, running a simulation, writing a report). Models and results are saved inside that workbook. Stochast has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own on the internet.

Nothing is sent anywhere

The Stochast pane is served by a small program on your own Mac (at localhost), and the pane is blocked from contacting any other address. It does not collect usage statistics, crash reports or analytics, and it does not check for updates. The Office.js library it ships with tries to load Microsoft telemetry; the pane blocks that too.

What others may see

  • Excel itself is Microsoft's software and follows Microsoft's privacy statement, whatever add-ins you use.
  • This download page and the installer files are hosted on GitHub, which may log visits and downloads under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Help and feedback in the pane opens a GitHub page in your browser. Anything you post there is public and is handled under GitHub's terms.

What the installer changes

It adds Stochast to /Library/Application Support/Stochast, starts its local server when you log in, adds Stochast to Excel's add-in list, and trusts a certificate that can only be used for your own Mac (localhost). Uninstall-Stochast.pkg removes all of it. Your workbooks are not touched.
